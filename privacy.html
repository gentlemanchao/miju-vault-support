Privacy Policy

Miju Vault · Effective September 11, 2026

On-device privacy

Miju Vault is an offline personal vault. Photos, videos, files, notes, file names, and passwords you add to the app remain on your device. We do not operate a server that receives, stores, or analyzes your vault content, and we do not collect personal data from the app.

Apple services

Annual subscriptions, lifetime purchases, and purchase restoration are handled by Apple. When you use these features, your device communicates with Apple under Apple's terms and privacy policy. Miju Vault does not send your vault content to us during a purchase.

Permissions

The app requests access to photos, files, or the camera only when you choose a related import or capture action. Miju Vault does not use location for tracking and does not collect location data.

Backups and recovery

Deleting the app, erasing its data, losing or damaging the device, or resetting the device may permanently remove local content. Export encrypted backups regularly and keep the backup files and their passwords in another reliable location. We have no copy of your data and cannot recover lost content or forgotten passwords. Restoring Apple purchases restores eligible membership rights only; it does not restore files.

Imports and exports

You may ask the system to delete original items after an import completes successfully. Originals remain if deletion is declined or cancelled. Items in Recently Deleted and cloud-synchronized copies are controlled by the operating system and your own services. Exported originals are outside the vault and should be handled carefully.

隐私政策

密屿 · 生效日期：2026 年 9 月 11 日

本地隐私

密屿是一款离线个人保险库。你添加到应用中的照片、视频、文件、备忘录、文件名和密码只保存在当前设备。我们不运营接收、存储或分析保险库内容的服务器，也不通过应用收集个人数据。

Apple 服务

年度订阅、终身购买和恢复购买由 Apple 处理。使用这些功能时，设备会依据 Apple 的条款与隐私政策和 Apple 通信；密屿不会在购买过程中向我们发送你的保险库内容。

权限

只有当你主动选择相应的导入或拍摄操作时，应用才会请求照片、文件或相机权限。密屿不使用定位进行跟踪，也不收集位置数据。

备份与恢复

删除应用、清除应用数据、设备丢失或损坏、恢复出厂设置，都可能永久删除本地内容。请定期导出加密备份，并将备份文件和对应密码保存在其他可靠位置。我们没有你的数据副本，无法恢复丢失的内容或遗忘的密码。恢复 Apple 购买只能恢复符合条件的会员权益，不能恢复文件。

导入与导出

导入成功后，你可以请求系统删除原件；拒绝或取消删除时，原件会保留。“最近删除”和云端同步副本由操作系统及你使用的服务管理。导出的原件已位于保险库之外，请自行妥善处理。
