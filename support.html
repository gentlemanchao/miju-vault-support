Contact

For product support, email mrcaoxiansheng@qq.com. Include your device model, iOS version, app version, and a description of the issue. Never send your vault password or backup password.

Back up your data

After unlocking a space, open Settings and choose Export Encrypted Backup. Save the backup outside the app, such as in iCloud Drive or on a computer, and confirm that the file exists at the destination. Keep the password separately.

Restore a backup

Backup restoration is available on the initial Create Your Private Space screen when the installation has no existing space. A backup cannot overwrite or merge with an existing vault.

Restore a purchase

Use Restore Apple Purchases on the membership screen to restore eligible subscription or lifetime purchase rights. This restores membership only and does not restore vault files.

联系我们

如需产品支持，请发送邮件至 mrcaoxiansheng@qq.com。请说明设备型号、iOS 版本、应用版本和问题现象。请勿发送空间密码或备份密码。

备份数据

解锁空间后，在“设置”中选择“导出加密备份”。请将备份保存到应用外部，例如 iCloud Drive 或电脑，并确认目标位置中已存在备份文件。密码请另行妥善保管。

恢复备份

当当前安装尚未创建任何空间时，可在初始的“创建你的私密空间”页面恢复备份。备份不能覆盖现有保险库，也不能与现有内容合并。

恢复购买

在会员页面使用“恢复 Apple 购买”，可恢复符合条件的订阅或终身购买权益。该功能只恢复会员权益，不恢复保险库文件。
